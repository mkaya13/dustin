Dustin: plan the close of a Stellar account

This page only plans. It never asks for a secret key; closing happens in your terminal or your own code.

Dustin (@mkaya13/stellar-dustin) is a TypeScript SDK and CLI that closes messy Stellar testnet accounts with fee-bumped, sponsor-paid transactions. Paste an account and this page runs the SDK's read-only planner in your browser: GET requests to Horizon, nothing signed, nothing submitted. It shows the ordered plan the CLI would print.

The public address of a classic account: 56 characters starting with G. Public addresses only; a secret key is never needed to plan.

Where the account's XLM goes when it is merged. Without a destination the page still inspects the account and lists the cleanup it can see, but the plan is BLOCKED: a merge needs a destination that exists on the ledger.

The funded testnet account that would pay every fee at close time. The plan names it as the payer and checks the fee bid against the sponsor's per-close budget (5 XLM). Without it the fees are still estimated from Horizon's fee statistics, and the payer is shown as fee_sponsor.

Options

The example is the builder's baseline fixture on the testnet: a messy account at its minimum balance (four trustlines with dust, one of them sponsored, two open offers, one data entry, zero spendable XLM). Planning reads it and changes nothing.
